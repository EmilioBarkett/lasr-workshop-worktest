# Claims log template (shared, traceable format)

Use one of these per paper. Keep it in the team's shared research drive/repo, not
in a chat history that only one person can see — the whole point is that three
weeks from now, anyone on the team can check what the paper actually said instead
of re-deriving it from memory or re-reading the whole paper.

```
# Claims Log: [Paper title, authors, year, link]
Extracted by: [name]  |  Verified by: [name]  |  Date: [date]

| # | Claim (plain language) | Quote (verbatim) | Scope | Type | Caveats | Verification status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | "..." | ... | stated finding | ... | VERIFIED |
| 2 | ... | "..." | ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Notes for the team
- Anything that needs a "don't cite claim #N without also citing the caveat in
  row M" warning goes here.
- Anything marked SCOPE ERROR or MISSING CAVEAT during verification should be
  fixed in the table above, not just noted here — this table is what people will
  actually read later.
```

Five minutes of overhead per paper, once. The payoff isn't on the first read —
it's three weeks later, when someone on the team is writing the paper's related-
work section from memory and would otherwise silently reproduce whatever
generalization slipped through the first ad hoc summary.
